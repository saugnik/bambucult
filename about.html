About BambooCult

We supply bamboo products to businesses: toothbrushes and toothpaste, shaving and personal care, handcrafted desk and home pieces, gift sets, and bamboo building materials.

01 — Who we are

Bamboo, without the sermon.

BambooCult works with retailers, hotels, dental clinics, offices, gifting companies, architects and builders. We keep the range practical: things people use every day, gifts they keep, and materials that hold up on site.

Everything is sold in trade quantities, and prices depend on how many you need and whether you want your own branding. So there are no prices on this website. You tell us what you need and we send you a quote.

Many of our bamboo pieces can carry your logo. Hotels put their name on guest toothbrushes; companies brand desk sets, bottles and gift boxes for staff and clients.

    03 — Notes on bamboo

    Why bamboo at all.

    Bamboo is a grass, not a tree. A cut culm grows back from its roots, so nothing needs replanting, and a stand can be harvested again within a few years.

    It is strong for its weight, which is why it shows up in everything from toothbrush handles to scaffolding and roofing. A bamboo handle breaks down in soil; a plastic one stays around for centuries.

    One honest note. The bristles on our toothbrushes are nylon, not bamboo. Pull them out with pliers before you compost the handle.
