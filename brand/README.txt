BambuCult logo - which file goes where
logo.svg            light backgrounds (documents, print, PDFs, design tools); culm B #8a6430, rest ink #1c221d
logo-reversed.svg   dark backgrounds and photographs; B #e3d2b1, rest paper #f3efe6
logo-one-colour.svg stamps, laser engraving, invoices, one-colour print; all #1c221d
logo-inline.svg     website only, pasted inline in the header and footer (B follows --bc-accent, the rest currentColor)
logo-1600.png       WhatsApp profile, email signatures, Word documents (1600 x 260 px, transparent)
mark.svg            the B alone (avatars, packaging, engraving); favicon.svg, favicon.ico and apple-touch-icon.png stay in the site root
Sizes: website header 24 px tall on desktop (22-24), 18 px on mobile (18-20), footer 22 px; never below 14 px tall or 5 mm in print. The logo is 6.15 times wider than tall (viewBox 0 0 516.33 84), so 24 px tall is 148 px wide.
Clear space: keep at least half the logo's height free on every side (12 px at 24 px tall); the mark alone keeps a quarter of its height.
Colours: ink #1c221d, culm #8a6430, paper #f3efe6, culm soft #e3d2b1, leaf deep #213726 (favicon tile).
Always use these outlined files, never the name typed in a font. The type is Libre Caslon Text (SIL Open Font License) redrawn as outlines.
