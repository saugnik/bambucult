# BambooCult · Culm monogram

The B is drawn as a length of bamboo culm: its stem is ringed by two nodes (a hairline cut with the slight ridge a real node has), its ends flare like a culm sawn just above a joint, and its two bowls are lighter, like split halves bent against the pole. The C is the same culm seen in section, a ring split open along one straight line, and its mouth spans exactly the internode between the B's two nodes, so both letters come from one construction rather than sitting side by side by accident. In two colours the culm is leaf green and the section is the tan of cut cane (green outside, tan inside); in one colour it still reads as a sturdy, plain BC. The letterforms are high-contrast and serifless, with optical corrections (overshoot on the C, a heavier round stroke, lighter bowls, bracketed joins), so the mark sits comfortably beside the Libre Caslon Display wordmark and the catalogue's printed, hairline-ruled look, like a maker's colophon rather than an eco badge. For 16 and 32 px a separate grid-fitted cut (favicon.svg) puts the stem, bars and nodes on whole pixels, and the two-colour mark is best kept at 24 px and above, with the one-colour version used below that.
