# Circle of Culms (geometric)

Three culms make one circle: a cult is a following, and a clump of bamboo is exactly that, culms growing together in a ring, so the circle is built out of the culms themselves rather than drawn as a frame around a bamboo picture. The mark sits on a 16-unit grid: a 14-unit disc split into three 4-unit culms by 1-unit cuts, with the centre culm whole and each outer culm carrying one node 2.5 units off centre, turned 180 degrees from each other so the rhythm rises left to right. Every cut lands on whole pixels at 16, 32, 48 and 96 px, and a single favicon.svg is hinted to stay sharp at both 16 and 32 px. In the lockup the wordmark (IBM Plex Sans Medium, 0.15 em tracking, light kerning on OO, OC and LT) is sized so its cap line meets the top of the right node and its baseline the foot of the left node: the name sits exactly between the nodes. It is one flat colour with no leaf, brush or frame, so it is as quiet as the catalogue's hairline rules and still holds up as a culm-soft disc in a browser tab. The lockup reads three CSS variables (--bc-mark, --bc-ink, --bc-accent), so the same inline SVG works on paper, reversed on ink or in one colour.
