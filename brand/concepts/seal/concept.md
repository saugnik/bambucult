# Maker's seal

BambooCult signs its goods the way a workshop does: the full seal is a round stamp edged with a thick-and-thin printer's rule, carrying BAMBOOCULT over BAMBOO GOODS in capitals drawn at one stroke width with flat ends, as a branding iron or engraving tool would cut them. Round letters overshoot, and the smaller bottom line is drawn slightly lighter so both lines look equally dark. At the centre is a cut culm, three segments split by node gaps, the top sliced off at 30° and two narrow leaves hanging at 40° from alternate nodes, built only from straight cuts so it reads as made rather than illustrated. That sprig is also the simplified mark, drawn on a 16-pixel grid so its culm edges and node gaps land on whole pixels at 16, 32, 48 and 96 px (and in the lockup at 30 and 60 px tall), which keeps it sharp in the favicon and header where the ring lettering would fill in. In the lockup it sits beside BAMBOOCULT in Libre Caslon Text capitals, tracked 0.16 em and kerned by hand, so the name speaks with the site's editorial Caslon. The seal is kept for packaging, labels, invoices and engraving on products, where culm brown makes it look burned into bamboo, while leaf green and ink carry the website.
