# Things to confirm before the site goes live

The product descriptions were written to be true for any reasonable supplier, and anything
uncertain says "on request". Confirm these with your supplier, then I (or you) can put the
real details into `assets/js/products.js`.

## 1. Your contact details — do this first

`assets/js/config.js` still holds placeholders. Until you replace them, the WhatsApp and
email buttons go nowhere.

- WhatsApp number with country code (e.g. `919876543210`)
- Phone number as you want it shown
- Email
- Address (or tell me to remove it)
- Working hours
- GST number, if you want it in the footer

## 2. Oral care

- **Toothpaste, tablets and tooth powder:** full ingredient list, whether it contains
  fluoride, the licence it is made under (cosmetic or Ayurvedic), whose brand is on the pack,
  shelf life and pack sizes.
- **Kids' toothpaste:** flavour and the age guidance printed on the pack. This is a sensitive
  category — nothing like "safe to swallow" goes on the site without the label saying it.
- **Toothpaste tablets:** jar material, tablet count, and whether refills are offered.
- **BC-110 and BC-111** are both "Natural (Pack of 4)". What is the difference? If there
  isn't one, we should merge them.
- **BC-120 "Dino"** is filed under adult brushes. Is it a kids' brush?

## 3. Bamboo Care

- **Wheat Straw Shaver:** disposable, or replaceable head? Wheat-straw handles usually contain
  a plastic binder, so the site does not call it plastic-free.
- **Shaving gel:** tube or bottle, sizes, and whether it is the same gel as in the razor pack.
- **Hair brush:** paddle, oval or round, and what the pins are made of.
- **BC-212 Green Box** is a cutlery kit sitting on the Care shelf. Move it to Bamboo Craft?

## 4. Bamboo Craft and Gifts

- **Table clock:** analogue or digital. **Desk calendar:** printed cards in a bamboo stand?
  **Diya holder:** what it looks like. **Lunch box:** steel or glass inner.
- **Travel tumbler:** stainless-steel inner (not bamboo fibre or melamine)?
- **Engraving:** check every item marked engravable can take a legible logo, and tell me the
  minimum order quantity for engraved goods.
- **Gift sets:** exact contents, the box type, how the box is branded, whether a message card
  is included, and a last order date for Diwali.

## 5. Bamboo Constructions

- Species, treatment method, and the sizes you can supply.
- Resin and grade for mat board, wall panels, flooring and roofing sheets.
- **Do not publish load, fire, water or termite ratings without test certificates.**
