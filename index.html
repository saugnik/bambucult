      06 — About us

      Bamboo, without the sermon.

      BambooCult supplies bamboo products to businesses: retailers, hotels, dental clinics, offices, gifting companies and builders. We keep the range practical. These are things people use every day, gifts they keep, and materials that hold up on site.

      Bamboo is a grass, not a tree. Some species grow close to a metre in a day, and a cut culm grows back from its roots, so nothing needs replanting. A bamboo handle breaks down in soil; a plastic one stays around for centuries.

      One honest note. The bristles on our toothbrushes are nylon, not bamboo. Pull them out with pliers before you compost the handle.
